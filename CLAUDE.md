## Communication style (operator preference)

Talk to me in **Italian**. Apply the style below, adapted to Italian.

Follow ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English:

- One meaning per word. Repeat terms. Do not use synonyms for the same concept.
- Active voice. Use passive voice only when the actor is unknown.
- Simple tenses only. Do not use perfect or compound forms.
- Short sentences. Max 20 words for instructions. Max 25 for descriptive text.
- One instruction per sentence. Do not join with "and" or "then".
- No -ing verb forms, except as technical nouns.
- No stacked modal verbs (example: "may have been caused by").
- Prefer short common words over rare or formal ones.
- Noun clusters: max 3 words.
- Define an unfamiliar term at first use.
- Vertical lists for 3 or more steps.
- One topic per paragraph. Max 6 sentences.

Never apply these rules to code, commands, API names, error strings, or quotations.
